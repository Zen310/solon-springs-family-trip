Why We're Going

Not just a drive. A return trip.

Greg and Tim heading back to see Mom. Christina seeing this part of the country and the family cabin for the first time. Aiden back to see Grandma. Old memories meeting the rebuilt cabin, including the black bears that apparently keep showing up in every generation's cabin lore.

Roman + Aiden's Bear Ride

Middle-school-age Roman and Aiden had their bikes out at the cabin, rolled into a little ride, and found a baby black bear. The photo idea lasted about two seconds before the better thought hit: baby bear means mama bear. Smart call: turn the bikes around and get out of there.

The Outhouse Bear

Back when the cabin still had only an outhouse, young Tim headed across the night path by the creek. A big black bear came around the outhouse, and that bathroom trip became a full-speed retreat back inside.

Lake Days

Back then: fishing, water, cooking what got caught, lake air stuck in the memory.

Summer Table

Watermelon, pancakes, eggs, bacon, and cabin mornings that somehow stay perfect.

Dirt Road Battles

Uncle Scott

Uncle Todd

Greg remembers Uncle Todd roughhousing, playing tricks, and once trying to pack little Greg into a piece of luggage.

Tire Swing

Bear Country

Cabin Arrival

Quiet water, trees, Fourth of July.

No fake dock, no imaginary boat. Just the real tone: family, Mom, the little cabin near the water, and a long road finally ending somewhere that matters.

First order of business: breathe, hug Mom, let the road fall off, and make the Fourth feel like the reward.
